Reward Notes

On Normal and Hard Mode, all weapon rewards will have 0%. On Super Hard, the following weapons have a +30 Accuracy Adjustment: 12-Ouncer, Bloom Shower, Chronos Scythe, Goof Swatter, Schwarz Stihl, and Twin Mustard. The number of slots an armor reward has will vary depending on the difficulty. In Normal Mode, an armor has 2 slots, Hard Mode has 3 slots, and Super Hard has 4 slots.
